# ADR-0001: CROIP is the source of truth for ratepayers, properties and concessions

Status: **Accepted by the owner, 2 Nov 2026.**

## Context

The Framework v1.0 (8 Oct 2026, §1) gives "registration and identity" to the concessionaire ("maintain customer and revenue accounts"), with the central platform only mapping approved identifiers. The iDARMS Golden Template Guide (10 Oct 2026, §3) likewise describes an iDARMS Property Registry that maintains "the authoritative property record".

## Decision

1. Ratepayers and properties are **primary government databases**. CROIP is the single source of truth for ratepayer identity and for every property in the country.
2. Concessionaires still register ratepayers and properties through their own systems, but the command is sent **directly to CROIP's API** and committed there. CROIP then publishes events, and each concessionaire receives a **synced copy** of only the ratepayers and properties in its concession.
3. **Billing and assessment** are generated by the concessionaire, then synced to CROIP for assurance.
4. **Payments** are collected by the concessionaire, and transaction data is reported to CROIP for oversight.
5. CROIP handles financial reconciliation, fraud detection, audit, case management and **concessionaire management** (onboarding, assignment, API credentials).

6. **Two-level onboarding (added 2 Nov 2026).** CROIP onboards concessionaires, their concessions, MMDA assignments and API credentials. Field agents, supervisors and all other local staff are onboarded only at the concessionaire. CROIP sees an opaque staff reference at most.
7. **Concession, concessionaire, assemblies (settled 2 Nov 2026).** A concession has one or more assemblies (MMDAs). The concessionaire is the entity that manages and represents the concession. Onboarding a concession in CROIP requires a concession admin (technical meaning open, O25).

## Why the model changed from the Framework

The Framework (8 Oct 2026) was a fully federated draft: concessionaires held registration and identity. Ratepayers and properties are national government records, so CROIP must be the single source of truth, which also lets CROIP detect duplicates and fraud across concessions. Billing and collection stay distributed because they are operational and concession-specific. The result is a hybrid: identity centralised, billing and collection distributed, oversight central. Framework §1, §4 and Figure 1 should be updated to match (CROIP-side task).

## Consequences for iDARMS

- iDARMS property and ratepayer services are **replicas plus a registration proxy**, not systems of record. Their charter's "Owned data" is replaced by "replica of CROIP data, read-only".
- The Golden Template Guide's Property Registry charter is an example of the *method*. Its ownership claims do not apply here.
- Replica keys are CROIP IDs. Local codes are secondary.
- Registration requires CROIP availability. Offline behaviour needs an explicit policy (OPEN_QUESTIONS O6).
- Framework statements that conflict with this ADR are overridden by it. The deviation should be raised with the consortium and Government, because the Framework is still a draft for their review.
